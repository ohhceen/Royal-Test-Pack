# QA 5: visual, content and hosting readiness (domain build)

What was tested: `deploy/jay/royalepandawa-site/index.html` served at http://localhost:8767/ (the build going to royalepandawa.com). It is identical to `royale-10k/index.html` except lines 13–14 (og:url and og:image already point at royalepandawa.com), so line numbers below are valid for both files.

Fixes were checked on `qa/qa5/patchedB.html`, which is the deploy build plus every fix below, served in place of `/`. The two proposed assets are routed in from `qa/qa5/visual/proposed/`. The full diff is in `qa/qa5/visual/proposed/patchedB-vs-deploy.diff`.

Scripts are in `qa/qa5v/` (`lib.mjs`, `shots.mjs`, `shotsfix.mjs`, `typo.mjs`, `a11y.mjs`, `verify.mjs`, `probe2/3/7.mjs`, `weight.mjs`, `dark.mjs`, `stitch.py`, `sheet.py`). Screenshots are in `qa/qa5/visual/`:
- `<vp>-v-<section>.png`: real viewport with the nav and concierge visible.
- `<vp>-s-NN-<section>.png`: section stitched from viewport captures, with fixed UI hidden.
- `<vp>-film-{top,tour}-NNN.png`: film frames at band midpoints.
- `fix<vp>-*`: the same shots on patchedB.
- `*-orig.png` / `*-fix.png`: before and after crops.

Contact sheets: `qa/qa5/visual/sheet-1440.jpg` and `qa/qa5/visual/sheet-375.jpg`.

Test setup: mp4 files were routed to the webm stubs, Spotify to a stub, and fonts were served (or blocked, where stated). An element `locator.screenshot()` resizes the viewport, which caused fake artefacts: a visible skip link, a collapsed spaces strip, smooth-scroll ghosts. All section shots were therefore stitched from instant-scroll viewport captures.

  - Bedroom crops show the beds: tour b2 and b3, room-suite, gal-royalin-7 and 8, the sp-bedroom thumbnail.
  - The sauna shows its interior (cedar benches behind glass, candles in front) in the spa grid, the space 03 thumbnail, the tour b4/b5 frames and the static still. It is not a vase close-up.
  - The door is frontal, with no stretch at 1440 (393x720), 1280x720 and 375 (qa4 fix in place).
  - The gym (`gym.webp`, `sp-gym.webp`) shows a bench, dumbbell racks, lanterns and a mirror, with no treadmill.
  - The weddings day images, kids room, sizes renders and plan renders are framed correctly.
- **Films:**
  - Posters and the first frame of all 10 legs, and all 6 static end stills, match their captions (`../tmp/posters.jpg`).
  - Static fallbacks show at 768x1024, 390, 375, 320 and 812x375 with 0 mp4 requests.

  - The arch finale is clean at p 1. Each viewport gave 0 console errors. The only "failed" requests are `blob:` range re-reads cancelled by the media element, which is benign.
- **Layout:**
  - No horizontal overflow at any of the 9 viewports, original or patched.
  - The nav CTA keeps a gutter of 16px or more.
  - The music panel and concierge stack above the nav and content (qa4).
  - "Ask Amira" never overlaps a CTA. It overlaps body text only as a normal floating button and is hidden over the hero.
