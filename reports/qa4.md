## Passed

- Door at ≤980: order is heading, lede, door, hold button, caption at 980, 768 and 375; button and caption are centred.
- Hold: pointer (1440 and 375), CDP touch (375) and Space (1440 and 375) all work. `--p` reaches about 0.4 at 600ms, returns to 0 on release and reaches 1 at 1.5s. The ring draws (`stroke-dashoffset` 88 → 0). Leaf ends at -108°, label reads "Welcome to your courtyard", 9 spaces light up, 0 errors.
- Nav: at 1281, 1300, 1366, 1440, 1536, 1600 and 1920 the 5 links sit on one line (25px each) with 64–75px gaps to the brand and to `.navr`. Links are hidden at 1280 and 1024.
- Music: `#mBtn` opens `#mPanel` with the Spotify iframe (152px). Escape closes it and returns focus to `#mBtn`. Outside click and button toggle close it; a click inside the panel does not. At 375 the panel is fixed at 16–359, top 80; at 320 it spans 16–306; at 565, 600, 1024 and 1440 it stays inside the viewport, over both the transparent and the solid nav. 0 errors.
- Royalty: 12 arches (6 gold sold, 6 outline). Bar is `scaleX(0)` before entry and animates to 50% (204/417px) after `.in`. "6 sold / 6 remaining"; no overflow at 1440 or 375.
- Finance: two cards. Australia and Dubai links set `#bFin` and land `#book` at 72. The request text contains "Finance introduction: Dubai (UAE)". Calculator AED: "AED 1,028,300" gross and "AED 514,150" share; price 850,000 → 16.5% and 6.1 years; switching back to USD gives 140,000.
- Concierge: KB answers for "finance", "dubai", "how many are sold", "How many villas are left?" and "Are any still available?" are correct. The greeting is spoken once with voice on, not spoken with voice off, and the chat works. The voice toggle sets `aria-pressed` and `localStorage amiraVoice`, and the setting persists across reload. A throwing `localStorage` causes no error. `speechSynthesis` undefined or deleted: `#vVoice` is hidden, `speak()` is a silent no-op, 0 errors. Mic is hidden when SpeechRecognition is absent. A fake SR result is asked and answered. A constructor that throws, or `not-allowed`, hides the mic with a message.
